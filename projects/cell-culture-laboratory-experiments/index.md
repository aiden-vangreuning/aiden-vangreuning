## Overview
This project involved hands-on laboratory work in cell culturing and introductory tissue engineering techniques, conducted within a **CL2 biosafety laboratory environment**. The work focused on developing practical competence in live cell handling, microscopy and experimental analysis across multiple biological systems.

Experiments emphasized proper laboratory technique, data-driven observation and clear technical documentation; forming a foundational skill set for biomedical research and wet-lab engineering applications.

To illustrate the scope and quality of the laboratory work, a representative lab report is provided below as a sample of experimental execution, data analysis and technical documentation.


{: .notice--info}
**Project Type:** Academic Laboratory Portfolio  
**Duration:** One academic term  
**Environment:** CL2 Wet Laboratory  
**Focus Areas:** Cell Culture · Microscopy · Biomaterials · Experimental Analysis  
**Role:** Experimental execution, data analysis and technical reporting

---

## Experimental Scope
Over the duration of the course, multiple structured laboratory experiments were conducted to investigate cellular structure, function and behaviour under varying experimental conditions, including:

- Optical microscopy and cellular morphology analysis  
- Membrane permeability and transport behavior  
- Cytoskeletal structure and function  
- Phagocytosis and cellular uptake mechanisms  
- Yeast cell cycle observation and analysis  

Each experiment required controlled preparation, observation and post-experiment data interpretation.

---

## Laboratory Techniques & Practices
Throughout the project, strict laboratory protocols were followed to ensure experimental validity and safety:

- Aseptic technique and contamination control  
- Live cell handling and preparation  
- Proper use of biosafety cabinets and laboratory equipment  
- Adherence to CL2 biosafety standards  
- Accurate experimental documentation and reporting  

Collected data were analyzed and summarized through structured technical lab reports.


{: .notice--primary}
**Key Skills & Competencies Developed**
- Practical cell culture and wet-lab handling techniques  
- Microscopy-based observation and analysis  
- Experimental protocol execution under biosafety constraints  
- Data-driven technical reporting  
- Attention to laboratory safety and reproducibility
